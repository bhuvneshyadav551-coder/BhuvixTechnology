GOOGLE SHEETS GUIDE

Connecting Your Google Sheet

Follow these steps to connect your dedicated spreadsheet and keep your personal files completely separate:

1. Dedicated Drive Folder

Create a folder in Google Drive named BhuvixTechnologies_Data. Keep your website sheet inside this folder so your personal documents are 100% isolated.

2. Create Spreadsheet Tabs

Create a spreadsheet named BhuvixTechnologies_Database with tabs: Services, Projects, and Careers.

3. Deploy Form Webhook

In Google Sheets, go to Extensions > Apps Script, paste the provided google-apps-script.js, and deploy as a Web App (Anyone can access).

4. Update js/config.js

Paste your Spreadsheet ID and Web App URL into js/config.js. The website connects instantly!
